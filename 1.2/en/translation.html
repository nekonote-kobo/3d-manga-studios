    Translation Dictionary

    3D Manga Studios supports Japanese and English through Blender's translation feature. Buttons, messages, tooltips and shader setting names switch automatically to match the Blender interface language (Preferences › Interface › Translation).

    This page lists every entry in the add-on's translation dictionary. Use it to look up what a word on the English screen is called in Japanese, or the other way around.
