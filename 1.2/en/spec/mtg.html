    Manga Toon Ground (Ground) NEW

    MTG · Material › Manga Toon Ground

    A material made just for the ground under your characters. It draws the shadows of the key, fill and back lights, each switchable on or off per light, as a flat shadow color (screentone can be used too). You can also make the ground transparent so only the shadow lies over the background, or fade out its edges so it blends into the background.

    Layer stack

    The texture, bumps, shadows and AO are multiplied over the ground color.

    2Set the edgeShadow Softness plus the Shadow Blur width make the shadow crisp or soft
    3MultiplyGround color × texture × bump shade → bump highlights → shadows (per light) × AO

    Only the shadow lights light the ground

    The ground gets three "ground shadow lights" per light group, with the same direction, color and strength as the key, fill and back lights. Regular lights do not light the ground (they are excluded with Light Linking), so raising Shadow Blur (the shadow light angle) does not change the character's shadows.
